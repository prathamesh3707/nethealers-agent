## Energy use and efficiency of AI-managed networks
<a id="energy"></a>

**Question.** Are there obligations or incentives for energy efficiency and sustainability of telecommunication networks and the AI systems that manage them?

**Where our agent meets it.** The agent may not switch cells off; an energy-saving mode (sleeping spare capacity at quiet hours) is a planned extension.

**What the host country's documents say.** The TRAI Recommendations acknowledge that AI can be used to optimize network performance and improve energy efficiency, thereby reducing carbon emissions in telecom networks [P1]. However, there is no explicit mention of obligations or incentives for energy efficiency or sustainability specifically for AI systems managing telecom networks. The focus is on collaboration and infrastructure development for AI adoption rather than regulatory mandates on sustainability [P2] [P5].

**Gaps.**
- *legal coverage*: While TRAI recognizes AI's potential to improve energy efficiency and reduce carbon emissions, there are no clear legal obligations or incentives for energy efficiency and sustainability of telecommunication networks or the AI systems managing them in the national policy documents [P1] [P2] [P5].
- *implementation*: Although AI's role in energy optimization is acknowledged, there is no mechanism or policy framework described to implement energy-saving modes or sustainability measures in AI-managed networks [P1].
